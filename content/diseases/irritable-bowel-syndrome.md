# Irritable Bowel Syndrome

Irritable bowel syndrome (IBS) is a common reason for abdominal discomfort.

**Symptoms**

- Abdominal pain gets relieved after defecation

- Looser stool during pain onset 

- More frequent stool at onset of pain

- Feeling of incomplete emptying

- Abdominal distension

- Mucus during rectum

**Alarming features**

IBS does not present with the following manifestations and is suggestive of alternate pathology 

- Blood in stool

- Family history of Inflammatory bowel disease

- Fever

- Weight loss

- Chronic diarrhoea

- Progressive dysphagia

**Diagnosis **

Patient with IBS-specific symptoms in absence of alarming symptoms indicates IBS

**Treatment**

- Antispasmodics

- Antidiarrhoeal

- Antidepressants and anxiolytics

- Laxatives

- Antibiotics
