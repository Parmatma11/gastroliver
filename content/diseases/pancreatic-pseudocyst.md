# Pancreatic pseudocyst

Pancreatic pseudocysts are a collection of fluid surrounding the pancreas, commonly seen in chronic pancreatitis patients compared to acute pancreatitis.

**Symptoms **

- Abdominal pain

- Jaundice

- Vomiting

- Fever, if there is infected pseudocyst

- Bleeding cyst

**Treatment **

Asymptomatic cysts should be regularly monitored.

- Cysts that reduce in size progressively, do not require any additional intervention.

- Persisting enlarged pseudocysts should undergo endoscopy or EUS guided drainage.

- Cysts difficult for endoscopic drainage require percutaneous drainage
