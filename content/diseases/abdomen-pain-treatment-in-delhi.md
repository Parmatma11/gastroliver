#### **Acute Causes**

- 

Acute appendicitis (infection in the appendix)

- 

Acute pancreatitis (infection in the pancreas)

- 

Perforated peptic ulcer

- 

Gallbladder inflammation due to stones

- 

Intestinal obstruction or perforation

- 

Diverticulitis or mesenteric ischemia

- 

Renal or ureteric stones

Gynecological factors (ectopic pregnancy, ovarian tumors)

#### **Chronic Causes**

Indigestion and bloating

Irritable bowel syndrome (IBS)

- 

Chronic pancreatitis or cholecystitis

- 

Peptic ulcers

- 

Abdominal tuberculosis

- 

Inflammatory bowel diseases (Ulcerative colitis, Crohn’s disease)

- 

Tumors or fluid accumulation in the abdomen
