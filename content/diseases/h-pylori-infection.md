# H Pylori Infection

H pylori is a bacteria that causes chronic infection within the stomach. A large portion of Indian population is affected from H pylori infection.

**Majority of individuals remains asymptomatic, however other complications include:**

- Gastric lymphoma

- Duodenal and gastric ulcer

- Gastric adenocarcinoma

**Diagnosis**

- UGI is indicated

- Biopsy is done from gastric antrum for Rapid Urease Test

**Treatment**

- Proton pump inhibitors 

- Antibiotics such as amoxicillin, clarithromycin, metronidazole and tinidazole
