# Acute pancreatitis

Acute pancreatitis involves pancreatic inflammation in a sudden manner presenting as abdominal pain, vomiting, abdomen distension and constipation.

Patients with severe disease may have decreased urinary output and respiratory distress.

**Common causes **

- Gall bladder stone

- Excessive alcohol intake

- Increased amount of blood lipids

- Hypercalcemia

- Hypertriglyceridemia

- Hepatocellular carcinoma (HCC)

- Drugs

- Tumor in pancreas or ampulla

- Worms in pancreatic duct

**Diagnosis**

Diagnostic tests of acute pancreatitis are:

- USG abdomen

- Serum Amylase and Lipase

- CECT abdomen

**Treatment**

Patients with acute pancreatitis may require immediate hospitalization, and treatment includes:

- Adequate IV fluids

- Organ support indicated in case of organ failure

- IV antibiotics indicated in infection

- Surgery or endoscopic/percutaenous drainage in case of infected collection
