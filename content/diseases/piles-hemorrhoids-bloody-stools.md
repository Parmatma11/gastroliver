# Piles/ Hemorrhoids (Bloody stools)

Inflammed and swollen blood vessels around the anus are referred as piles. It usually results in bleeding, discomfort while sitting and itching.

**Common causes are**

- Strained bowel movements

- Chronic diarrhea

- Chronic constipation

- Pregnancy
