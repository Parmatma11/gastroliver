# Achalasia

Achalasia is an esophageal disorder, where lower esophageal sphincter is impaired due to motor dysfunction.

**Symptoms**

- Dysphagia

- Chest pain

- Weight loss

- Odynophagia

- Aspiration pneumonia

**Diagnosis**

Diagnosis of achalasia includes:

- Upper GI endoscopy for identifying obstructing lesion

- Barium swallow

- Esophageal manometry

**Treatment**

Treatment options include:

- Surgery

- Botulinum injection

- Oral nifedipine and nitroglycerine

- Endoscopic pneumatic balloon dilatation

The main treatment modalities include Surgery and endoscopic dilatation.
