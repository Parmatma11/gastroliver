## Understanding Intestinal Gas

**Intestinal gas** forms naturally in the digestive tract as food breaks down. Swallowing air while eating, consuming carbonated drinks, or eating too quickly are common **reasons for intestinal gas**. However, excessive gas can also be caused by food intolerances (like lactose or gluten), imbalance in gut bacteria, or gastrointestinal conditions such as [IBS (Irritable Bowel Syndrome)](https://www.mayoclinic.org/diseases-conditions/irritable-bowel-syndrome/symptoms-causes/syc-20360016).

When **gas inside intestines** is unable to move normally, it can cause abdominal pressure, discomfort, or even sharp cramps—often described as **intestinal pain and gas**. If left untreated, it may lead to bloating, belching, and a feeling of heaviness after meals.

## Common Intestinal Gas Symptoms

If you frequently feel bloated or experience uneasiness after eating, you might be suffering from **intestinal gas symptoms** such as:

- Abdominal bloating and distension 

- Sharp cramps or pressure in the lower abdomen 

- Excessive burping or flatulence 

- Nausea or heaviness after meals 

- Pain caused by **gas trapped in intestines** or **small intestine gas trapped** for long periods 

If these symptoms persist for more than a few days, it’s best to consult a [**gastroenterologist in Delhi**](https://gastroliver.in/gastroenterology/) to rule out chronic digestive conditions.
