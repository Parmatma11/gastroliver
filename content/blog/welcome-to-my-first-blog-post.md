# Welcome to My First Blog Post

Dec 23, 2021 | [Blogs](https://gastroliver.in/category/blogs/), [Medical](https://gastroliver.in/category/medical/) | [0 comments](https://gastroliver.in/welcome-to-my-first-blog-post/#respond)

				I am Dr. Ankita Gupta a gastroenterologist in Delhi. I have got expertise in treating diseases and disorders of stomach, esophagus, pancreas, biliary systems, liver, rectum, colon, and also gastroesophageal reflux disorder. I have completed my M.B.B.S from Christian Medical College, Ludhiana India in the year 2007 and MD in Medicine from Christian Medical College, Ludhiana India in the year 2012. I have done D.M Gastroenterology from Dayanand Medical College, India in the year 2016 and also training in GI motility: American Neuro gastroenterology and Motility Studies from Medical College of Georgia United States of America. I have completed my fellowship in advanced endoscopy and luminal gastroenterology from Sir Ganga Ram Hospital, New Delhi in the year 2016 – 2017. I have attained Gold Medal in my University Examination, DM Gastroenterology in Dayanand Medical College. My thesis was done in M.D. Medicine: Lipid profile in rheumatoid arthritis and its correlation with the disease activity and D.M. Gastroenterology: Endoscopic and Clinicopathological profile of isolated terminal ileal ulcers.

People come across many stomach problems in their life. The stomach is the main part of our body that helps in digestion. When your stomach is affected, your body will not be able to get all the nutrients that it needs for energy. I am a doctor and through my blog want to let people be aware of the stomach disorders and how to treat those problems with the right treatment. Many cases come to me regarding the gastric problem and many of them come in severe stages. In my profession, I have seen people ignoring their digestive problems and seeking medical advice later at severe stages. It is because of this I have started blogging so that people will be aware of the different disorders and about right treatments at the right time. I want to radiate my knowledge through this platform. My blogs will focus on gut health. Remember when your gut is healthy, your body will be in a good state. No one should ignore any issues of the stomach and liver. If you observe any symptom then you can take my appointment.

My mission is to care for my patients and their families that matters most to me.

My vision is to invent the future of healthcare.

My values are collaboration, excellence, accountability, and respect.

My aim to write blogs is to make you aware that how important is your digestive system and keeping it in a healthy state should be your priority. My blog will put the focus on digestive health and I hope it will enlighten you and keep you happier, and healthier. If I will enlighten people through my blog then I will feel a huge success.

On our site, you will find informative postings about gastroenterology and our practices. We believe that if patients are educated then they are better prepared to make decisions about the health of their digestive system. Our blogs are designed to provide you with the latest developments regarding gastroenterology and valuable health advice through our dedicated team. We hope you find our blog to be a source for having an up to date knowledge with proper digestive health care and treatments.

We here welcome all comments and questions.
