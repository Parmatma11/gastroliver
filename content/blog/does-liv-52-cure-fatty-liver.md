## **What Is Fatty Liver Disease?**

Fatty liver disease occurs when fat accumulates in liver cells, which can affect liver function over time. This condition often develops silently, with no early symptoms, and may progress to more serious liver problems if left untreated.

### **Types of Fatty Liver**

- NAFLD (Non-Alcoholic Fatty Liver Disease): Fat accumulation caused by obesity, insulin resistance, or metabolic syndrome without alcohol consumption.

- Alcoholic Fatty Liver: Liver fat buildup due to excessive alcohol intake, often accompanied by liver inflammation.

### **Why Fatty Liver Is Dangerous**

If left unmanaged, fatty liver can progress to serious complications such as:

- NASH (Non-Alcoholic Steatohepatitis), which involves inflammation and liver cell damage

- Cirrhosis, where irreversible liver scarring occurs

- Liver failure, which can be life-threatening

Early detection, proper lifestyle interventions, and medical monitoring are crucial for preventing these outcomes.
