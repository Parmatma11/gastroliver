## What is Mounjaro (Tirzepatide)?

Mounjaro is a once-weekly injectable medication that targets two key hormones:

- GLP-1 (Glucagon-Like Peptide-1)

- GIP (Glucose-Dependent Insulinotropic Polypeptide)

These hormones regulate appetite, blood sugar, and fat metabolism.

## How Mounjaro Helps in Weight Loss

- Reduces hunger and cravings

- Slows gastric emptying

- Improves insulin sensitivity

- Promotes fat loss

**Clinical results:** Patients can lose up to 15–22% of body weight with proper medical guidance.

## Who Should Consider Mounjaro?

- BMI ≥ 30 (Obesity)

- BMI ≥ 27 with diabetes, PCOS, or fatty liver

- Struggling with diet and exercise alone

## Benefits of Mounjaro

- Significant weight loss

- Better blood sugar control

- Improved metabolic health

- Reduced fatty liver risk

## Side Effects of Mounjaro

### Common:

- Nausea

- Vomiting

- Constipation or diarrhea

- Loss of appetite

### Rare but Serious:

- Pancreatitis

- Gallbladder disease

- Thyroid-related risks
