## **Refer below top 5 things that cause stomach pain.**

### **Gastritis**

Pain occurs in the region of stomach lining becomes inflamed or swollen. This condition is common in people from alcohol to NSAID (Non-Steroidal Anti-Inflammatory Drug) use. The infection causing bacteria is H.pylori and the common symptoms that have seen are vomiting, bloating, gas, nausea etc. Gastritis is one of the top 5 causes of stomach pain.

### **Esophagitis**

The esophagus is the food-pipe or tube that carries food from mouth to stomach. This cause occurs when the lining of the esophagus becomes inflamed and irritation to be continued course of action and can be prolonged without consult to the doctor.

### **Gastroesophageal Reflux Disease**

Gastroesophageal Reflux Disease (GERD) is a condition in which acid leaks and move back into the esophagus, causing the burning sensation in the chest or throat. Leaking of an acid is a long-term condition that involves persistent acid reflux and included symptoms are heartburn, abdominal pain, and nausea, hoarseness, cough or lump in the throat.

### **Pancreatitis**

A symptom when inflammation occurs in pancreas and pain in the upper belly region. The pancreas is a small gland that releases insulin to control blood sugar levels. Sometimes pancreatic stones are also seen in the pancreas which can also lead to abdominal or [stomach pain](https://www.instagram.com/drankitagupta/). Alcohol and gallstone disease are the two most common cause of pancreatitis.

### **Irritable Bowel Syndrome (IBS)**

IBS is a gastrointestinal disorder that includes abdominal pain, cramps, excessive gas, bloating and changing in bowel (feces) time. This might be occurring due to hormonal changes, stress, eating habits. A more going undiagnosed condition which can be challenging and treatment options are far and not satisfactory. It is only based on the cleaning of the abdominal tract region especially need to change eating habits.
