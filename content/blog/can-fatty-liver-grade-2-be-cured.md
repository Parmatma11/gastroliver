## **What is Fatty Liver Grade 2?**

Fatty liver occurs when excess fat accumulates in liver cells. The condition is categorized into three stages:

**Grade 1**

- Mild fat accumulation

- Minimal symptoms

- Highly reversible with lifestyle changes

**Grade 2**

- Moderate fat accumulation

- Noticeable changes in liver function tests

- Still reversible with proper care

**Grade 3**

- Severe fat accumulation

- Possible inflammation and liver damage

- Harder to reverse, higher risk of fibrosis or cirrhosis

Grade 2 represents a **critical turning point**. At this stage, the liver still retains the ability to regenerate, but intervention is necessary to prevent further damage. Patients are often asymptomatic or experience mild discomfort, making medical guidance essential.
