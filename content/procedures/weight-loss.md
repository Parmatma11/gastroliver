## Comprehensive evaluation, before we treat anything

At GLEC, no treatment begins without understanding your body in detail. Every patient undergoes a structured medical evaluation &mdash; because liver health, inflammation and metabolism directly influence how your body stores and burns fat.

      01 · BLOOD
#### Complete Blood Analysis

A full metabolic and nutritional blood workup to establish your baseline.

      02 · INFLAMMATION
#### Inflammatory Markers

Measured to detect low-grade inflammation that slows fat loss.

      03 · HORMONES
#### Hormonal Assays

Thyroid, insulin and reproductive hormones that govern weight gain.

      04 · GENETICS
#### Genetic Attribute Analysis

Insight into how your body individually responds to diet and therapy.

      05 · LIVER
#### Advanced FibroScan

Precisely quantifies liver fat, central to metabolic weight gain.

      06 · COMPOSITION
#### Body Composition (BCA)

Assesses visceral and subcutaneous fat, not just weight on a scale.

## Frequently asked questions

      What is the best weight loss treatment?The best weight loss treatment is medically supervised, personalised, and addresses metabolism, liver health, hormones and lifestyle together.
      How is medical weight loss different from regular dieting?Medical weight loss identifies and treats root causes such as hormonal imbalance, fatty liver and metabolism issues, rather than just reducing calorie intake.
      How does fatty liver affect weight loss?Fatty liver slows metabolism and fat processing, making it harder to lose weight without medical treatment.
      What tests are required before starting treatment?Blood analysis, hormonal studies, a liver scan (FibroScan), and body composition analysis.
      Are GLP-1 and GIP therapies effective?Yes — when prescribed by specialists, these therapies help regulate appetite and improve metabolic function.
      What is an endoscopic balloon for weight loss?A minimally invasive procedure where a balloon is placed in the stomach to reduce appetite and food intake.
      How long does weight loss treatment take?Duration varies, but noticeable results usually begin within a few weeks with consistent monitoring.
      Can weight loss treatment improve digestion?Yes — improving gut microbiome and liver health enhances digestion and nutrient absorption.
      Is the weight loss permanent?With proper lifestyle changes and follow-up care, results can be long-lasting and sustainable.
      Why choose GLEC for weight loss treatment?A comprehensive, doctor-led program focusing on root causes — liver health, metabolism and hormonal balance — for safe, lasting results.
