# Liver Biopsy

Liver biopsy is a procedure used to determine various types of liver diseases. It is a safe procedure. Complication rarely occurs (1 in 50,000 procedures)

**Technique**

A small tissue piece is obtained from the liver to perform a microscopic examination.

**Indications**

- Idiopathic cause resulting in an increased level of liver enzymes 

- Staging of liver diseases such as chronic hepatitis B, autoimmune hepatitis, and Wilson disease

- Sampling of liver lesions
