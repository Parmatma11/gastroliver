# UGI Endoscopy

**Upper GI endoscopy is a procedure that evaluates:**

- The mucosa of esophagus, 

- stomach and 

- duodenum

**Technique**

- An Endoscope is introduced into the mouth after sedating the patient that helps to examine internal structures such as stomach, and esophagus.

- In case of any suspicion, a biopsy is suggested

- Endotherapy can be done for bleeding lesions.

**Advantages**

Endoscopy is an extremely safe procedure that does not cause any complications.

It is usually done on outpatient basis and patient can be discharged in 2-3 hours.

**Indications of UGI endoscopy**

- Bleeding (red or coffee colored blood) from mouth

- Difficulty in swallowing food

- Upper abdominal pain

- Chronic diarrhea

**Endoscopes for various therapeutic procedures:**

- Banding of varices

- Endotherapy of ulcers

- Polyp removal

- Foreign body removal

- Dilatation of strictures

- APC application for GAVE/PHG/PGP

- Stenting of esophagus and duodenum
